About BMCC

The Borough of Manhattan Community College (BMCC) is a public community college in New York City. Founded in 1963 as part of the City University of New York (CUNY) system, BMCC grants associate degrees in a wide variety of fields.

BMCC's original campus was scattered around midtown Manhattan. The current campus has been in use since 1983.
